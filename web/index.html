Mark three points on a frame where the plate and your feet are clear. They let us track the bar and find your midfoot. You can skip this; bar and foot checks will then show "cannot assess".

How to film it

  • Camera to the side, square to you, at hip height and level.
  • Whole body, feet and plates in frame for the entire set.
  • One lifter in view. Good light.
  • Any number of reps; trim to one set.

What we accept

  • MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV or AVI

Videos are processed on our server and kept only so you can reopen this report.
